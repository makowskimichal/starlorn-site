starlorn

adj.  /ˈstɑːr·lɔːrn/

feeling the distance in every letter you send, knowing that, like starlight, each word arrives long after it left you.

A quiet Gmail client

Starlorn is a small, local-first email client for Gmail on Android, Windows and Linux. It is built for a handful of people who want their mail without the noise.

  • Several Gmail accounts in one shared inbox
  • Read and act on mail straight from notifications
  • Swipe gestures for archive, delete and snooze
  • No Starlorn servers, no analytics, no AI processing: your mail stays between your device and Google
